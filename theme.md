## Fonts
- Display / titles: **Anton** (uppercase, tall, bold). Size up to 148px.
- Body: **Switzer**, fallback **Instrument Sans**.
- Labels: uppercase, letter-spacing .04em to .24em.

## Feel
- Dark, cinematic, photo backgrounds with dark overlay.
- Glass cards (white 10% fill, thin white line).
- Numbered sections: "01. Engines — We Build", "02. Products — We Own".
- Motion: ease cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), 0.35s.
- Shadows: 0 12px 32px -4px #0000001f.

## Words
- Tagline: "We build the Engines & the Worlds they power"
- "An integrated technology group" — Singapore & Bangkok.
- Engines (We Build): Magick Robotics, Magick Workforce, Magick Mind, Magick Studios, Magick Pro Services (soon).
- Products (We Own): Magick Box, Magick Learn, Magick Guardian, Fantastic Adventures, Matji and Co, Magick Tailor, OrgAI, GabiGo.
- Offices: Bangkok (True Digital Park West), Singapore (100 Tras Street).
